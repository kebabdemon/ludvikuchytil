Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

What data we access

When an account owner authorizes Clipper, the platform (YouTube, Instagram or TikTok) issues access tokens that allow Clipper to upload videos to that account. For TikTok this uses the video.publish scope and basic account information returned during authorization (such as the open ID).

How data is used

Tokens are used only to publish videos the account owner chose to publish. Clipper does not read private messages, followers' data or analytics, and does not sell or share any data with third parties.

Where data is stored

Access tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are never sent anywhere except to the respective platform's official API.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner revokes access. Access can be revoked at any time in the platform's settings (e.g. TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions), after which the stored tokens stop working and can be deleted.
